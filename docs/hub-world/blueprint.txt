[Title] danielavila.tech — Super Mario 64 Hybrid Portfolio Design Blueprint
Project Overview: An interactive 3D web portfolio inspired by the Peach's Castle hub world in Super Mario 64. The website uses a Hybrid Navigation Architecture that blends full third-person free-roam movement with one-click guided auto-navigation, allowing visitors to either play through the castle lobby or effortlessly cycle through doors and paintings to view education, technical skills, certifications, and career experience.
[Heading1] 1. Core Foundation & UX Architecture
[Heading2] 1.1 The "Press Start" Title Screen
[|li] Visual Presentation: Visitors land on a bold, chunky 3D "DANIEL AVILA 64" logo over either an interactive low-poly 3D bust (inspired by the Mario face screen) or a slow panoramic camera orbit of the castle courtyard.
[|li] Entry Trigger: Clicking the flashing "PRESS START" button plays the classic start chime and smoothly swoops the camera into the castle lobby behind the player avatar.
[|li] Audio Toggle: A persistent speaker icon in the corner lets visitors mute or unmute retro sound effects and background music at any time.
[Heading2] 1.2 The Low-Poly "Daniel 64" Avatar
[|li] 3D Model Specs: Approximately 600 to 800 triangles using flat/Gouraud vertex shading and crisp 64x64px nearest-neighbor textures for an authentic 1996 N64 aesthetic and instant browser loading.
[|li] Visual Design: Built on classic N64 proportions, customized with longer hair and dressed in either a sharp casual tech outfit or a stylized low-poly navy blazer.
[|li] Animation State Machine:
[|li] Idle: Subtle breathing bounce; glances left and right after 5 seconds.
[|li] Sleep (Easter Egg): After 20 seconds of inactivity, sits down and nods off with floating "Zzz" particles.
[|li] Run Cycle: Classic arms-out N64 sprint with small white dust puffs at the feet.
[|li] Door Push/Pull: Reaches forward to push castle doors open before entering an alcove.
[|li] Single Jump / Dive: Used when jumping around the lobby or diving into a painting canvas.
[|li] Backflip Exit: Plays when jumping back out of a painting overlay onto the lobby floor.
[|li] Star Pose ("Here we go!"): Triggered when interacting with the central Resume Star pedestal.
[Heading2] 1.3 The "Pause Menu" UX Safety Net
[|li] Instant Accessibility: Pressing ESC or clicking the persistent [ + PAUSE / MENU ] button in the top-right corner brings up the classic Super Mario 64 Pause Menu.
[|li] Fast-Travel & Resume Access: Allows recruiters, mobile visitors, or users in a hurry to immediately jump to any section overlay or download the standard PDF resume in a single click without needing to walk around.
[Heading1] 2. Spatial Layout: The Castle Foyer & Open-Alcove Design
To avoid slow multiple loading screens (opening a door, fading to black, walking into a second room, and jumping into a painting), the castle hub uses an Open-Alcove Architecture inside a single compact octagonal room:
[|li] The Main Octagonal Lobby: Features the iconic checkerboard floor, a red central carpet with a custom "DA" emblem, a skybox ceiling with clouds, and warm N64 vertex lighting.
[|li] The Five Labeled Doors: Arranged in a semi-circle around the main walls of the foyer. Each door features a floating 3D signpost and glowing Star emblem above the frame:
[|li] Education (Far Left — 1-Star Door)
[|li] Skills (Mid Left — 3-Star Door)
[|li] Career & Labs (Center — Big Star Door)
[|li] Certifications (Mid Right — 3-Star Door)
[|li] About & Contact (Far Right — 1-Star Door)
[|li] Seamless Door-to-Painting Reveal: Each door is recessed into a deep stone archway. Approaching a door (by walking or clicking) swings it open on its hinges with the classic door creak sound, immediately revealing the glowing Course Painting hanging inside the alcove.
[|li] The Center Pedestal: Located in the center of the red carpet with a wooden signpost and rotating Golden Power Star for instant access to contact links and the PDF resume.
[Heading1] 3. The Hybrid Control System
Both control schemes remain active simultaneously and transition seamlessly without requiring the user to pick a mode:
| Control Method | User Input | On-Screen Behavior |
| 1. Guided Door Cycling | Clicking < PREV DOOR or NEXT DOOR > UI buttons at the bottom of the screen, or scrolling the mouse wheel. | The avatar automatically turns and sprints along a smooth path to the selected door. The door opens and the camera frames the painting over the avatar's shoulder. |
| 2. Direct Point-and-Click | Clicking or tapping directly on any Door, Signpost, or Painting in the 3D scene. | The avatar sprints to the clicked door to open it. Clicking an exposed painting causes the avatar to triple-jump directly into the canvas. |
| 3. Full Free-Roam | Pressing WASD, Arrow Keys, Spacebar (Jump), or dragging an on-screen mobile thumbstick. | Any automatic pathing cancels immediately. The camera enters third-person follow mode, giving the visitor full manual control to run, jump, open doors, and leap into paintings. |
| 4. Pause Menu Override | Pressing ESC or clicking the top-right [ + PAUSE / MENU ] button. | Plays the pause chime and opens a clean 2D navigation menu with direct section links and a one-click PDF resume download. |

[Heading1] 4. Step-by-Step User Journey
[|li] Title Screen Entry: Visitor clicks "PRESS START" on the "DANIEL AVILA 64" title screen; the camera swoops down behind low-poly Daniel standing on the center emblem in the foyer.
[|li] Lobby Navigation: A subtle bottom HUD displays [ < Prev ]  LOBBY (Use WASD to walk or Click a Door)  [ Next > ].
[|li] Approaching a Door: Moving near or clicking a door triggers the proximity sensor, swinging the wooden door open to reveal the illuminated painting inside the alcove and displaying a top banner with the course title.
[|li] Jumping Into the Painting: Jumping into the canvas (Spacebar) or clicking the painting triggers a dive animation, a concentric WebGL liquid-ripple shader across the painting texture, the classic warp sound effect, and a brief white screen flash.
[|li] Reading the Course Overlay: A high-contrast, responsive HTML/CSS overlay styled after the SM64 "Act Select / Star Screen" opens, displaying crisp, selectable, and mobile-friendly portfolio text.
[|li] Jumping Back Out: Clicking [ JUMP OUT OF PAINTING (ESC) ] closes the overlay, triggers a reverse ripple, and ejects the avatar out of the painting with a backflip onto the castle floor outside that door.

[Heading1] 6. Technical Stack & Phased Development Roadmap
[Heading2] 6.1 Recommended Tech Stack
[|li] 3D Web Engine: Three.js (or React Three Fiber) rendering directly inside an HTML5 Canvas.
[|li] Custom Shaders: Lightweight GLSL WebGL fragment shader for the concentric water-ripple distortion when entering and exiting paintings.
[|li] 3D Assets: Compressed .glb models created in Blender (under 2MB total) using 64x64px nearest-neighbor textures.
[|li] UI & SEO Layer: Semantic HTML/CSS overlays for all course text and the Pause Menu so content remains crisp on all screens, copy-paste friendly, and indexable by search engines and resume parsers.
[Heading2] 6.2 Four-Stage Build Plan
[|li] Stage 1: Whitebox Prototype — Build the octagonal foyer using basic Three.js geometry, add a placeholder character capsule, and implement the Hybrid Controls (WASD free-roam + < Prev | Next > auto-pathing) and proximity door triggers.
[|li] Stage 2: Painting Ripple Shader & HTML Overlays — Implement the WebGL canvas ripple transition and build the responsive SM64-styled HTML/CSS course overlays for Education, Skills, Career/Labs, Certifications, and Contact.
[|li] Stage 3: Low-Poly Art & Avatar Integration — Replace whitebox shapes with textured N64 castle walls, doors, paintings, and the custom rigged "Daniel 64" .glb character model and animations.
[|li] Stage 4: Audio, Title Screen & Polish — Add the "Press Start" title screen, Pause Menu fast-travel modal, mobile touch controls, and optional retro sound effects.
